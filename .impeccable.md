## Design Context

### Users

FreezeDryMachine is used by factory operators at the machine computer and by
technical users reviewing the same process remotely. Their primary jobs are to
confirm that collection is running, understand the most recent 24 hours at a
glance, inspect a specific historical period when needed, and distinguish real
machine behavior from missing or malformed CSV data without losing raw records.

### Brand Personality

Industrial, dependable, precise. The interface should feel calm during normal
operation and unambiguous when attention is required. Dense process data is
welcome, but the product must earn trust through truthful labels, stable
behavior, and clear separation between live state, historical context, and
quality problems.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use a restrained industrial/utilitarian visual language inspired by physical
machine consoles: compact DIN-like typography, tabular numerals, cool tinted
metal surfaces, sharp alignment, modest corner radii, and amber as a deliberate
selection/warning accent. Preserve both light and dark themes. Avoid decorative
dashboard trends, oversized marketing typography, glass effects, gradients used
as ornament, and controls that hide essential monitoring context.

### Design Principles

1. Show the operator's immediate time window first; keep longer history one
   explicit action away and never discard it.
2. Preserve data truth: gaps, invalid readings, sentinel codes, and inferred
   states must remain visually distinguishable rather than being smoothed away.
3. Optimize for fast scanning at factory-display distance with compact controls,
   strong hierarchy, high contrast, and tabular values.
4. Keep critical controls available and understandable on desktop and narrow
   screens, with keyboard focus, pressed states, and non-color cues.
5. Use motion sparingly and only to explain state changes; respect reduced-motion
   preferences and prioritize stable charts.
